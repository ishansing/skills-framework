## Findings

1. **Run ledger field was misleading (fixed).** After ROUTE-01 completed research, the ledger
   recorded `current_phase: research`, so RESUME-01 had to reconcile a stale value. The ledger
   now uses `next_phase` (where a fresh session resumes) in `references/artifacts.md` and
   `idea-to-production/SKILL.md` step 7.
2. **Environment fidelity (not a framework defect).** The upstream `research` skill says to
   spin up a background agent; the subagent runtime does not expose a task tool, so research
   ran inline and flagged the adaptation. Main sessions have the task tool.
